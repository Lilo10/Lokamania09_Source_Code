Questions

Good to know

Do I need the Frost List to get in?

No. Entry is free and open to everyone at any time on both days. The list never affects getting in; it only adds extras.

Does it cost anything?

No. Signing up is free, and so are the perks.

Do I get every perk?

Everyone on the list gets the lineup first and access to the registrants-only channel. On top of that, each sign-up gets one Frost Pack with one surprise perk, and your welcome email tells you which one. One pack per person.

How do I claim my perk?

Head to the Lokamania booth on 6 or 7 Nov, show your welcome email and pass code, and collect your physical voucher. Then redeem that voucher for your perk. Showing the email alone won't get you the perk, so make sure you pick up your voucher first. The lineup and the channel link come to you by email.

Are the totes and sticker packs guaranteed?

They're given while stocks last each day, so come early to be safe.
