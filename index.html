Before you apply

Know the house rules.

♥Where and when is Lokamania 10?

Lokamania 10 takes place on November 26, 27 and 28 at Majarrah, Sheikh Zayed, over Black Friday weekend.

♠What is the deadline to apply?

November 12. Register through this website before then; applications submitted after November 12 can't be accepted.

♦Can I take part on only some of the days?

Yes. Choose Day 1, Day 2 or Day 3 only, Days 1 & 2, Days 2 & 3, or all three days, subject to approval and availability.

♣What spaces are available?

The Kiosk (1.5 m × 70 cm), The Booth (80 cm length × 1.5 m width), The Mega Cube (4 m width × 3 m length) and The Flagship (7 m width × 2.5 m length). The spaces you can choose depend on your category, and The Flagship is for Fashion—Clothing brands only.

♦Who can apply?

Local consumer brands may apply. The team reviews every application for event fit, product quality and category balance.

♣Does applying guarantee a space?

No. Spaces are limited and each application is reviewed before participation is confirmed.

♥Do I need Black Friday offers?

This edition is built around Black Friday, so brands are encouraged to bring launches, exclusive drops or special prices.

♠When is payment required?

Payment is requested only after the application is accepted and the space is confirmed.

♦When will I hear back?

The team will contact applicants after reviewing their application. Timing may vary with application volume.
