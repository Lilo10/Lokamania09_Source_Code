Frequently asked questions

Where and when is Lokamania 09?

The Winter Edition takes place on 6 and 7 November 2026 at U Venues.

Can I participate for only one day?

Yes. Request 6 November only, 7 November only, or both days, subject to approval and availability.

Who can apply?

Local consumer brands may apply. The team reviews every application for event fit, product quality and category balance.

Which spaces are available?

Fashion—Clothing: Small or Large Booth. Beauty: Kiosk. Fashion Accessories: Kiosk or Small Booth. Home & Lifestyle, Food & Beverages, and Art & Stationery: Kiosk. Other categories are reviewed individually.

Does applying guarantee a space?

No. Spaces are limited and each application is reviewed before participation is confirmed.

Can I launch a new collection at the event?

Yes. This Winter Edition is designed for brands launching or showcasing winter collections.

How are brands selected?

The team considers product fit, brand presentation, collection relevance, category balance, dates and remaining capacity.

Can two brands share one space?

Shared spaces require prior approval. Contact the Lokamania team before applying to discuss the proposed arrangement.

When is payment required?

Payment is requested only after the application is accepted and the space is confirmed.

Can I edit my application after submitting?

Contact the Lokamania team with your brand name and application reference.

When will I receive a response?

The team will contact applicants after reviewing their application. Timing may vary with application volume.
